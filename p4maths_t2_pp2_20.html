Question 20

The line graph shows the amount of water in the tank from 3 p.m. to 7 p.m. The tank was completely filled with water to the brim at 7 p.m.

The graph is called Volume of water.

(a) What was the capacity of the tank?
(b) At what time was the tank half filled with water?
(c) What was the increase in the volume of water from 5 p.m. to 6 p.m.?
Strategy
Read the words carefully Trace on the graph Check the answer form Count the small gaps
  • Do not choose the top number on the axis unless the graph point shows it.
  • For graph reading, follow the path: axis label → point → other axis.
  • For part (b), the answer is a time, not an amount.
  • For part (c), find the two readings first, then find the increase.
